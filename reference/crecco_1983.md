# Daily mortality of juvenile American shad

A built-in dataset containing estimated daily mortality rates for larval
and juvenile American shad in the Connecticut River, USA 1979-1982
(Crecco et al. 1983).

## Usage

``` r
crecco_1983
```

## Format

A data frame with 24 observations of 6 variables:

- `Year`:

  Year of study

- `Age`:

  Fish age (d)

- `Md`:

  Daily mortality

- `Sd`:

  Daily survival

- `Si`:

  Interval survival

- `Sc`:

  Cumulative survival

## References

Crecco, V., T. Savoy, and L. Gunn. 1983. Daily mortality rates of larval
and juvenile American shad (\*Alosa sapidissima\*) in the Connecticut
River with changes in year-class strength. Canadian Journal of Fisheries
and Aquatic Sciences 40:1719–1728.
