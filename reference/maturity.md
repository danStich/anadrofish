# Sex-specific maturity schedules for American shad

A dataset containing sex-specific probabilities of maturation at age for
each life-history region (NI = Northern iteroparous, SI = Southern
iteroparous, SP = Semelparous)

## Usage

``` r
maturity
```

## Format

A data frame with 6 observations of 15 variables:

- `region`:

  Life-history region

- `sex`:

  Fish sex

- `age1`:

  Probability of maturing at age 1

- `age2`:

  Probability of maturing at age 2

- `age3`:

  Probability of maturing at age 3

- `age4`:

  Probability of maturing at age 4

- `age5`:

  Probability of maturing at age 5

- `age6`:

  Probability of maturing at age 6

- `age7`:

  Probability of maturing at age 7

- `age8`:

  Probability of maturing at age 8

- `age9`:

  Probability of maturing at age 9

- `age10`:

  Probability of maturing at age 10

- `age11`:

  Probability of maturing at age 11

- `age12`:

  Probability of maturing at age 12

- `age13`:

  Probability of maturing at age 13
