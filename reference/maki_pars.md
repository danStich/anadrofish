# Sex-specific maturity schedules for River Herring

A dataset containing sex-specific probabilities of maturation at age for
each life-history region (SAT = Southern Atlantic, MAT = Mid Atlantic,
SNE = Southern New England, MNE = Mid New England, NNE = Northern New
England, CAN-NNE = Canada and Northern New England)

## Usage

``` r
maki_pars
```

## Format

A data frame with 216 observations of 6 variables:

- `Species`:

  Species of fish

- `Region`:

  Genetic reporting group and general geographic region

- `Sex`:

  Fish sex

- `Age`:

  Fish age (years)

- `pMature`:

  Probability of maturation at age

- `pMat.se`:

  Standard error of estimated pMature from ASMFC (2024)
