# Sex-specific instantaneous natural mortality of river herring

A dataset containing sex- and age-specific instantaneous natural
mortality estimates for each species and genetic reporting region

## Usage

``` r
mortality_rh
```

## Format

A data frame with 300 observations of 5 variables:

- `region`:

  Genetic reporting group region

- `sex`:

  Sex of fish

- `age`:

  Age in years

- `M`:

  Instantaneous natural mortality

- `species`:

  Species
